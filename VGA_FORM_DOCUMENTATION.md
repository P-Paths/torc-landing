## 🔄 **Form Variations Analysis**

### **Variation 1: "Injured Party" Focus**
- Uses "Injured Party Name", "Injured Party Email", "Injured Party Address"
- Focuses on the person who played video games
- Direct claim filing

### **Variation 2: "Caller" Focus**  
- Uses "Caller Name", "Caller Email", "Caller Address"
- Focuses on the person filling out the form
- Representative claim filing

### **Key Differences**
1. **Field naming**: "Injured Party" vs "Caller"
2. **Legal authorization**: Different requirements
3. **Contact information**: Primary vs representative
4. **Claim type**: Direct vs representative
